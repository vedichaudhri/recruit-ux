Paste your accomplishments in plain language, or upload PDFs — your resume, a LinkedIn export, work samples, whatever you've got. No formatting, no one-page limit. Your recruiting agent will read it, work out your strengths and gaps, and match you against every open role, instead of making you guess which of them applies to you.

① Add your Anthropic API key to activate the agent

This is a client-side prototype with no backend — your key is stored only in this browser's localStorage and sent directly from your browser to Anthropic's API. It is never sent to us or stored anywhere else. Don't use this pattern for a real product; get a key at console.anthropic.com.

Only needed if your key is scoped to your organization rather than a workspace (Anthropic will say so with an "anthropic-workspace-id" error if so) — find it on your workspace's settings page in the Console.
